# Crypto accumulation analysis

This tool studies spot accumulation using public market evidence; BTC and ETH are its default assets.
Ledgerly remains authoritative for actual transactions, holdings and acquisition costs.

## Market scope

**Asset universe**:
The explicitly selected set of asset identifiers whose native markets are monitored.
Membership does not establish provider availability or recommend an investment.

**Native market**:
An asset and quote pair on a named source, retaining that source's instrument identity.
The same ticker on another source does not by itself establish economic equivalence.

**Operating acceptance period**:
Actual elapsed observation of a declared collection workload, with its gaps,
failures and freshness retained; accelerated synthetic time is a separate experiment.

**Frozen validation protocol**:
A dated, immutable declaration of evaluation cohorts, event clocks, model versions,
cost assumptions and acceptance criteria fixed before eligible prospective issues.

## Purchase plans

**Native quote**:
The currency in which a source actually publishes a market's prices and volumes.
EUR, USD, USDT and USDC are distinct quotes.

**Contribution**:
Dated quote cash declared as available to a hypothetical purchase plan.
A future contribution cannot fund an earlier purchase.

**Tranche**:
A scheduled amount of quote purchase notional, excluding quote fees.
A policy can divide a tranche into immediate and deferred slices.

**Activation**:
The timestamp at which a scheduled tranche requests exclusive cash allocation.
Contributions and target reserve releases at that timestamp precede the allocation.

**Cash commitment**:
Quote cash exclusively assigned to a hypothetical purchase intent, including any
quote fees; it is unavailable to other intents until spent or released.

**Deferred target**:
A purchase intent below an already completed reference price, with a waiting
deadline and an explicit fallback.
A touched price alone does not establish an executable fill.

**Price-free funding preview**:
A conservative illustration of declared cash commitments without market prices,
fills, rounding refunds or inferred account balances.

**Shared budget envelope**:
One optional dated cash supply shared by declared asset plan families in one
native quote and data mode. Embedded plan contributions are not additional funds.

## Evidence and prospective analysis

**Captured evidence**:
The fixed set of source observations available to an analytical calculation at
its cutoff, retaining source, quote, mode and observation times.

**Known-at version**:
A version of a market observation available by the decision time under study.
Later corrections do not establish earlier availability.

**Original assessment**:
An immutable issued analysis with its actual issuance time, frozen request,
rules and consumed evidence.
_Avoid_: Mutable report

**Outcome**:
An appended observation of an original assessment's later results, with its own
observation time and evidence; it does not rewrite the original.

**Descriptive reference frequency**:
An observed event frequency in eligible, disjoint historical windows, with its
sample count and uncertainty.
It remains distinct from a supported conditional forecast.

**Prospective scorecard**:
A descriptive comparison of frozen assessments and later eligible observations,
with explicit cohorts, missingness and execution qualification. It does not turn
historical simulations into prospective performance.

**Indicative FX reference**:
A dated informational currency conversion reference with explicit source and
orientation, distinct from an executable exchange rate.
